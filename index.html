About

From heart monitors to production dashboards

I'm an Application Reliability Engineer at Visa, based in Wadsworth, Ohio. I keep production applications healthy across Kubernetes, Windows servers, and everything in between.

My path here wasn't a straight line. As a cardiac monitor technician, I watched rhythms for up to 63 patients at a time. As a Quality Manager at Superior Trim, I led a team auditing parts and tracking down nonconforming material. Both jobs taught me what reliability work demands: catch problems early, find the root cause, and stay calm when something breaks.

A Python course during the COVID lockdown got me hooked on code. I finished the We Can Code IT bootcamp in 2022 and joined CardinalCommerce, now part of Visa, that same year.

Off the clock, I'm usually spending time with my dog, playing strategy games, or getting a lift in.
